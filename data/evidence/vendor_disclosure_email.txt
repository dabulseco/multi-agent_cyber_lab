Subject: Security advisory - reportgen-utils 1.4.3 maintainer account compromise

We are disclosing that the maintainer account used to publish reportgen-utils
1.4.3 was compromised. The release included an unauthorized post-install
script that contacts an external host during installation.

Versions affected: 1.4.3 only
Versions safe: 1.4.2 and earlier, 1.4.4 and later (patched)
Known impact: the post-install script has been confirmed to perform network
callbacks in a subset of reported cases; we have not yet confirmed what data,
if any, is transmitted by the callback.

We recommend auditing any environment that installed 1.4.3 during the
affected window and upgrading immediately.
