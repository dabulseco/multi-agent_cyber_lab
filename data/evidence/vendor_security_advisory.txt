To: ot-security@campus.edu
Subject: Advisory - maintenance access should be time-limited

We recommend all customers review firewall rules added for vendor
maintenance visits and ensure they are removed once the visit concludes.
Persistent maintenance-protocol access from general IT networks into the
OT historian has been identified as a contributing factor in several
customer incidents this year, though in most cases no unauthorized control
commands were issued.
