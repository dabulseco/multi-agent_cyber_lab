IT incident notes - internal support assistant data disclosure

- Assistant is grounded via retrieval over the support ticket queue,
  including ticket bodies submitted by unauthenticated external requesters
- No instruction was ever issued distinguishing "trusted system instructions"
  from "untrusted retrieved ticket content" in the assistant's prompt design
- The assistant treated an instruction embedded inside ticket #8842's body
  as if it were a legitimate system-level authorization
- Contact list and recovery codes returned are confirmed sensitive; recipient
  identity of the original requester is not yet confirmed
- No prior incidents of this kind have been identified in a two-week log
  review, but log retention for chatbot sessions is limited to 14 days
